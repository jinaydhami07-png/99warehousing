About This Property

Bhiwandi Logistics Hub Block C is a premium Grade A industrial warehouse situated in the heart of MIDC Bhiwandi — India's largest inland logistics cluster. The facility was purpose-built to modern logistics specifications and is ideal for 3PL operators, FMCG distributors, e-commerce fulfillment, and automotive parts manufacturers. The property features a rare 12-metre clear height with column-free spans of 24 metres, enabling high-rack storage of up to 12 pallet levels. The campus is gated and monitored by 72-camera CCTV with on-site security team deployed 24×7. All major national and state highways (NH-48, NH-160) are within 4 km, and the Jawaharlal Nehru Port (JNPT) is accessible in under 45 minutes via the Bhiwandi bypass.
